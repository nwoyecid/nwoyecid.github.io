    <h1>Biography</h1>

    <p>
      <strong>C</strong>hinedu <strong>I</strong>nnocent <strong>D</strong>ominic popularly known as <strong>CID</strong> is an AI scientific researcher at <a href="http://icube.unistra.fr/en/">ICube laboratory</a> France, where he began in February 2018 with research focus on developing new deep learning applications in healthcare. He is also a post-doctoral research fellow at <a href="https://camma.unistra.fr/">CAMMA</a> <a href="https://www.ihu-strasbourg.eu/institut/presentation/">IHU Strasbourg</a>, where he leads pioneering efforts in Surgical Generative AI.
    </p>
    <p>
      His research interests include Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Machine Learning and their applications in surgical assistive intervention and human-machine cooperation during surgery. His research objectives point towards the future generation intelligent operating room (OR). He is the principal organizer of the MICCAI Endoscopic Vision Biomedical Challenge on CholecTriplet, a groundbreaking initiative that models surgical activities through the lens of <em>surgical action triplet</em> &mdash; a representation of (tool-action-anatomy) relationships innovated by his PhD thesis.
    </p>
    <p>
      In addition to his research contributions, Nwoye served as an Area Chair for <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/ipcai2024/organization" target="_blank">IPCAI 2024</a> and is a co-organizer of the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/lm-surg/organization" target="_blank">NeurIPS workshop on Large Models Meet Surgical Data Science</a>. Currently, Dr. Nwoye also serves as an adjunct lecturer (Enseignant vacataire) at the <a href="https://www.unistra.fr/index.php?id=english">University of Strasbourg</a>, where he shares his expertise and passion for advancing the field of surgical AI.
    </p>

    <h2>Education</h2>
    <p>
      He earned a <strong>Ph.D. in Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Medical Robotics</strong> from the <a href="https://www.unistra.fr/index.php?id=english">University of Strasbourg</a>, France. His thesis, which won the <a href="http://ed.math-spi.unistra.fr/doctorat/prix-de-these/">MSII doctoral thesis prize 2022</a>, focused on the use of deep learning techniques for the recognition and detection of surgical tools and fine-grained activities in endoscopic videos. This thesis was supervised by <a href="http://camma.u-strasbg.fr/npadoy">Prof. Nicolas Padoy</a> and <a href="https://www.chru-strasbourg.fr/praticien/mutter-didier/">Prof. Didier Mutter</a>, examined and defended before <a href="https://campar.in.tum.de/Main/NassirNavabCv">Prof. Nassir Navab</a> (TU Munich), <a href="https://www.nct-dresden.de/forschung/departments-and-groups/department-for-translational-surgical-oncology.html">Prof. Stefanie Speidel</a> (NCT Dresden), and <a href="https://www.artorg.unibe.ch/research/aimi/group_members/persons/prof_dr_sznitman_raphael/index_eng.html">Prof. Raphael Snitzmann</a> (University of Bern).
    </p>
    <p>
      In 2017, he was among the pioneer students of the <a href="https://cmmrs.mpi-sws.org/">CMMRS</a> pre-doctoral program at Saarbr&uuml;cken, Germany organized by Cornell University, University of Maryland, and Max-Planck Institute for Software Systems.
    </p>
    <p>
      He obtained his second <strong>M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence (with Distinction)</strong> from the <a href="https://www.southampton.ac.uk/">University of Southampton</a>, UK. His Master's thesis focused on machine learning methods for early diagnosis of skin cancers, supervised by <a href="https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~sm3y07/">Assoc. Prof. Sasan Mahmood</a> and <a href="https://www.ecs.soton.ac.uk/people/jsh2">Prof. Jonathan Hare</a>.
    </p>
    <p>
      He also obtained his first <strong>M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Computer Science (First Class Honours)</strong> from the <a href="https://www.unn.edu.ng/">University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN)</a>.
    </p>

    <h2>Career</h2>
    <p>
      In 2022, he started a post-doctoral research at <a href="http://camma.u-strasbg.fr/">CAMMA</a> headed by <a href="http://camma.u-strasbg.fr/npadoy">Prof. Nicolas Padoy</a>, in close collaboration with the <a href="https://www.ihu-strasbourg.eu/institut/presentation/">IHU MixSurg institute</a>, <a href="http://www.ircad.fr/?lng=en">IRCAD</a>, and the Strasbourg University Hospitals. He also belongs to the wider research groups <a href="https://rdh.icube.unistra.fr">RDH</a> and <a href="http://icube-web.unistra.fr/dsai/index.php/Main_Page">DSAI</a> in ICube.
    </p>
    <p>
      He is also a lecturer at <a href="https://www.unn.edu.ng/">UNN</a> in the department of computer science, where he began as a graduate teaching assistant in 2014. He was a Google ambassador between 2010&ndash;2012 and founded the Software Developers Club (SDC) and <a href="https://cidsoft.com">CIDSoft</a> in Nigeria.
    </p>
